Our Story

Blackwood & Bean began in 2023 as a small coffee stall at a weekend farmers' market. Using locally roasted beans and a hand-me-down espresso machine, founder Lerato Mokoena built a loyal following among market regulars. By 2024, steady demand made it possible to open the first physical location. In 2025, Blackwood & Bean expanded into a full-service restaurant, introducing breakfast, lunch, and dinner menus while keeping its coffee program at the centre of the brand.

Our Mission

Blackwood & Bean exists to turn the everyday ritual of eating and drinking coffee into something worth slowing down for. We source our beans locally and roast them and prepare everything on-site. More than the food itself, our mission is hospitality: every person who walks through our door should feel welcome to stay as long as they like.
